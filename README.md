# FL HOA Notice Generator

Free, client-side notice generator for self-managed Florida HOA boards.
Drafts violation notices (§720.305), late-assessment notices (§720.3085),
and meeting notices (§720.303) from fill-in fields — everything runs in your
browser, nothing is uploaded anywhere.

**Not legal advice.** Drafts require board review and, where appropriate,
review by a Florida-licensed attorney before sending.

Live: https://phillipmex.github.io/fl-hoa-notice-generator/
